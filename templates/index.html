All moves are measured from official closes, starting at the engulfing day's close — so +1d matches the next day's move as charted. Cumulative shows the running change since the trigger; Daily shows each day's individual move. Overview tiles always use cumulative moves; med (median) is the headline because it resists outliers like ARAI's −30% runs, with the raw average beside it. Entry is the price when the alert fired (~30 min before the close), for reference. Signals are tracked for 10 trading days (~2 weeks), then frozen. Live shows the current move for still-tracking signals while the market is open. Colors reflect price direction, not win/loss — a bearish signal "wants" red.
